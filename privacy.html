Privacy

Last updated 16 November 2026

We keep this short because we collect very little.

What this site collects

Reloceans does not use cookies, ad trackers or accounts. You can read everything here without telling us who you are.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub records technical data such as your IP address when you visit, to keep the service running and secure. You can read how GitHub handles this in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to download them. See the Google Privacy Policy.

If you write to us or join our list

If you email us, we use your address only to reply. If you join our email list, we use your address only to send Reloceans news, and every email has an unsubscribe link. We never sell or share your address.

Social media

When you follow us on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube, those platforms handle your data under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time. Write to hello@reloceans.com and we will answer within 30 days.
